# Viewing images in the browser

Open a study in the built-in viewer: window/level, zoom, pan, scroll through series, measure.

### 1. Open a study

1.  In Studies, press View on a study row (or 'View images…' in its ⋯ menu).
2.  The viewer opens in a new tab so the study list stays where it was.

In the console: `/studies`

### 2. Pick a series

1.  The left column lists the series with a thumbnail, modality and image count.
2.  Click one to show it. The left / right arrow keys also switch series.

### 3. Scroll, window, zoom

1.  Mouse wheel (or ↑ ↓, PgUp/PgDn) steps through the images.
2.  The slider at the right does the same.
3.  Left drag adjusts window/level (left-right = width, up-down = centre), right drag or Ctrl+wheel zooms, middle drag pans.
4.  CT presets (lung, bone, soft tissue…) are in the Window list; 'Window: as sent' restores the modality's values.

### 4. Orientation and tools

1.  Invert, rotate, flip, Fit and 1:1 are on the toolbar.
2.  Measure draws a distance using the pixel spacing stored in the image (approximate, not for diagnostic use).
3.  Multi-frame images and series can be played with ▶.
4.  Press ? for the full key list.

### 5. What it can show

1.  Grey-scale and colour images of any modality, including compressed transfers (JPEG, JPEG-LS, JPEG 2000, RLE).
2.  They are decoded on the server.
3.  Reports, PDFs, presentation states and raw data are listed in Studies but skipped by the viewer.
4.  Every study opened is recorded in the Audit Log (Web, then View).
